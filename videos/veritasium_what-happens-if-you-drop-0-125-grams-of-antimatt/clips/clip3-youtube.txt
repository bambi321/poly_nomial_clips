Could you really steal antimatter from CERN?

In Angels and Demons, thieves steal an eighth of a gram of antimatter from CERN, but a CERN scientist works out that the factory has only made about a trillionth of a gram in 25 years. At that rate, making an eighth of a gram would take longer than the age of the universe. Annihilating a whole year's worth would only warm a millilitre of water by about one degree.
