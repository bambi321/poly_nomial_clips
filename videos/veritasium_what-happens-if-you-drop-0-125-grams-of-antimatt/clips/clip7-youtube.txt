The physicist robbed of a Nobel Prize

Lee and Yang won the Nobel Prize in Physics for discovering parity violation. But Chien-Shiung Wu, whose experiment proved it, was left off the prize and never honoured by the committee. Fellow Nobel winner Jack Steinberger called it the biggest mistake in the Nobel committee's history.
