Bananas contain a tiny amount of radioactive potassium-40, and roughly every 75 minutes one of those atoms releases a positron, a particle of antimatter. To match the output of CERN's antimatter factory, you'd need about a billion bananas. Your own body has trace radioactive atoms too, so one estimate says you make around 180 positrons an hour.
