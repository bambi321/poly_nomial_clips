Most people live in the mid-latitudes, the band of land where the temperature suits us and our main crops grow best. Much of the world's food comes from just a few regions there, like the US Great Plains and Ukraine. In a worst-case nuclear winter, these regions could stay below freezing for years, leaving the world's bread baskets empty.
