The countries that might survive a nuclear winter

Every country with nuclear weapons is in the Northern Hemisphere, so that's where a nuclear winter would hit hardest. Countries like Australia, New Zealand and Argentina may cope better, thanks to a milder winter and plenty of livestock. But survival isn't guaranteed, especially if starving nations come looking for their food.
