As the story goes, Claude Shannon tested how predictable English is by asking his wife Betty to guess a book letter by letter. He kept only the letters she got wrong and replaced correct guesses with dashes. A perfect copy of Betty could rebuild the whole text from that shorter version, showing how predictability allows compression.
