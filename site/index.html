Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Who we are

Polynomial is a small, independently run science clips channel. "poly_nomial clips" is the private tool we use to publish our own videos to our own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (through the YouTube API Services): uploading videos to our own channel, and reading our own channel's videos and their statistics.
  • TikTok and Instagram: publishing videos to our own accounts and reading their basic account information.

The only accounts the tool signs in to are the ones we own. It does not collect, request or store information about anyone else, including viewers, followers or the creators we clip.

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own computer, in files that are not shared or uploaded anywhere. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google and YouTube

By using YouTube features through this tool, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page.

Deleting data

Revoking access from Google or TikTok's account settings stops the tool immediately. On request we delete any stored tokens and data within 7 days.
